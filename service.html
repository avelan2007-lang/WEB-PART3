Our Programmes

At Greensleeves Children's Trust, we provide a holistic approach to child development. Our programmes are designed to nurture the mind, body and spirit of every child in our care.

Education for All

We run an on-site schooling structure supporting children from babies up to Grade 12. Our educational approach focuses on:

  • Early childhood development for infants and toddlers
  • Primary and secondary education up to Grade 12
  • Individualised learning plans for each child
  • Extracurricular activities including sports and arts

Life Skills Development

We believe in equipping children with practical skills that will serve them throughout their lives. Our life skills programmes include baking, bricklaying, permaculture gardening, and financial literacy.
